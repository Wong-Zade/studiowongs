Wongs Digital Arts is an independent graphic design studio working across branding, print, and digital.

Every project starts on paper and finishes by hand — logo systems and layouts are built and refined in Affinity Designer and Affinity Publisher, so the details hold up whether they're on a screen or a printed page.

The studio takes on a small number of projects at a time, working closely with each client from first sketch to final files.
